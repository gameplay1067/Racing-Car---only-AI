What could not be completed:
The actual Ultimate Racing page imports Three.js 0.160.0 from jsDelivr, with unpkg as fallback. The container could not resolve the external CDN host, and Chromium's local-page navigation was blocked by the environment policy. Therefore the actual Three.js game scene could not be loaded and played end-to-end in this container. The WebGL2 probe confirms that Chromium can create a working WebGL2 context under SwiftShader, but it is not proof that the entire game renders correctly or achieves 60 FPS on the user's GPU.

7) PHASE 6 FIXES APPLIED
------------------------
- AI decisions now read one same-frame traffic snapshot; no rival is updated using an inconsistent mix of old/new positions.
- AI-to-AI contact handling re-checks up to five separation passes, applies lateral separation and makes the trailing vehicle yield when lateral movement is blocked.
- Player lateral movement is clamped inside a safe road envelope that accounts for car width.
- Minimap path geometry is cached instead of regenerated each frame.
- Garage preview renderer is lazy-initialized to avoid allocating a second WebGL context at initial load.
- Adaptive quality lowers DPR / shadows after sustained low FPS and restores them after stable FPS windows.
- Browser tab visibility / blur clears held inputs and pauses the race.
- A visible fallback message is now shown when WebGL initialization fails.
